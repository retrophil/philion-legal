High Infamy Privacy Policy

Developer: Philion  ·  Draft prepared: 18 November 2026

Pre-release draft — not yet a final policy. Details about advertising SDKs, final store purchasing, precise retention periods and account deletion must be confirmed against the release build before this page is submitted to Google Play.

Philion is the developer of High Infamy. This page explains the kinds of information the game is designed to process, why it is needed and how players can contact us about their data.

1. Contact

For questions about privacy, access to your data or account deletion, contact Philion at philiongames@outlook.com.

2. Data associated with your game account

Based on the current Unity and Firebase implementation, High Infamy uses or stores:

  • Authentication information: a Firebase user identifier and, where you register or sign in using email, the email address associated with your authentication account. Anonymous account authentication is also supported in the reviewed code.
  • Player profile: your chosen player name, avatar, support identifier, character appearance, tutorial and profile-completion state.
  • Gameplay and progress: progress and unlocks, jobs attempted and completed, game results, in-game achievements and performance records.
  • Virtual economy and inventory: in-game bankroll, PP coin balances, reputation, owned in-game items, market-trading data and related transaction records.
  • Social gameplay information: some player names, avatars, scores, rankings and in-game activity can appear to other players through the game's leaderboard and public activity feed.

When you contact support, we also process the information you choose to send us, such as your email address and account-support details.

3. How data is used

We use this data to create and authenticate accounts, save and restore progress, operate the virtual economy and game systems, display leaderboards and activity, deliver support, investigate suspected abuse, and maintain the security and reliability of the service.

4. Service providers and public visibility

High Infamy uses Google Firebase services, including Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore and Cloud Functions, to provide sign-in, profile storage and game backend features. These services may process technical connection information as necessary to operate and secure the service. Google's service data practices are explained in its Firebase privacy and security documentation.

Information you submit as your player name, avatar, activity or game ranking may be shown to other players through the game's public ranking and activity features. Do not include personal information in a player name.

5. Purchases and payments

If paid bundles become available, purchases will be processed by the relevant mobile platform store (such as Google Play or Apple's App Store). The game backend may receive purchase or transaction evidence, product identifiers and entitlement records so that it can verify the purchase and provide the purchased in-game content. We do not need your complete payment-card number to grant game rewards.

Release review needed: the final store purchase implementation, its fraud-prevention data and any legally required transaction retention must be confirmed before launch.

6. Advertising and analytics

Release review needed: the audited source does not establish the final advertising or analytics provider, whether personalised advertising is used, or what device identifiers those providers may process. This section must be completed, with any relevant provider links and consent information, before a build containing ads or analytics is distributed. Do not interpret this draft as a declaration that no ads or analytics will be present.

7. Security and storage

We use authenticated access and server-managed game operations for player records and virtual currency. No online system can guarantee absolute security. The game may also store some player settings or cached game information on the device.

Firebase and app-store service providers may process information in locations outside the United Kingdom. Applicable data-protection safeguards and vendor terms need to be reflected in the final policy.

8. Retention and account deletion

Player records are generally needed while an account is being used. You may request deletion of your High Infamy account and associated personal data by following the account-deletion instructions. We may need to confirm that the account belongs to you before fulfilling a deletion request.

Some limited records may need to be retained where legally required, such as payment or fraud-prevention records. Release review needed: the exact deletion workflow, retention schedule and exceptions must be documented and implemented before launch.

9. Your choices and rights

You can contact Philion to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal data associated with your game account. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to restrict or object to certain processing, or to make a complaint to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO).

10. Updates to this policy

We will update the policy if High Infamy's features, service providers or data practices change. The version published for release must include its effective date.
