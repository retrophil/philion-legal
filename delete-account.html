High Infamy / Player accounts

Request account and data deletion

Pre-release draft. The in-game account-deletion entry point and backend deletion procedure still need to be implemented and tested before High Infamy is published on Google Play.

Philion is the developer of High Infamy. If you want to delete your player account and associated game data, you can contact us from outside the app using the instructions below.

How to make a request

  1. Email philiongames@outlook.com with the subject High Infamy — Account Deletion Request.
  2. Tell us the player name and, if available, the game's support ID shown on your profile. If the account is registered with an email address, you can use that email address to contact us.
  3. We may need to verify your account ownership to prevent someone else from deleting your account.
  4. After verification, we will explain the deletion process and confirm when the applicable account data has been removed.

Do not send your password, SMS codes, payment-card numbers or recovery codes. If you cannot access the game, you can still use this email-based request method.

Data covered by an account deletion request

The process is intended to cover the High Infamy authentication account and associated player profile, saved progress, virtual currency, owned in-game items, game records, market transactions, and any public leaderboard or activity information that identifies the account.

Some records may have to be kept where the law requires it (for example, certain purchase, tax or fraud-prevention records). A verified deletion request is not a request to refund purchases or transfer in-game items.

In-game deletion

The final version of High Infamy must also offer a readily discoverable way to start account deletion from inside the game. This feature is still being implemented and has not yet been verified.

Other privacy requests

For requests to access or correct your personal data, or for any questions about your account, contact philiongames@outlook.com.
